## ETc from ET0
The water a crop uses under good conditions (ETc) is the reference evapotranspiration (ET0, from weather) multiplied by the crop coefficient: ETc = Kc x ET0.

## The Kc curve
Kc changes along the crop cycle in four stages: the initial stage (Kc ini, constant), the crop development stage (Kc rises in a straight line from Kc ini to Kc mid), the mid-season stage (Kc mid, constant, usually the highest) and the late season stage (Kc falls in a straight line from Kc mid to Kc end). Day by day, during development and late season, Kc is found by moving along the line between the value at the end of the previous stage and the value at the start of the next one.
